# Step 24 part 5: review of Gemini's plan (AI equipment purchases)

The plan is approved with these corrections. Write the edits from it.

1. **The need per family.** `forcesOf(n)[f].need` does not exist. The AI's shortfall is the local `need` object built in `function aiForces(n, us)`, keyed by land equipment type (`EQK`), just before `aiLines(n, need, war);`. Store it on the nation there (`n.eqNeed = need;`, right before that call) and read it in `aiEqBuy`. A family's shortfall is `max(0, need[k] − (n.eq[k] || 0))` for land kit; for `air` and `shp` use `anGap` as `aiLines` does, or leave air and ships out of this part (preferred: land families only).
2. **Supplier checks.** Use `eqWants(n, S.id, f, m, q, price, false)`; it returns `''` when the buyer agrees, otherwise a reason. It already refuses war, sanctions, poor relations, and a buyer that can't afford `price + 2 months of revenue`. On the supplier's side, skip any supplier that is human, is at war itself, has posture `'defend'` or `'war'`, or has less than two lots of the model in stock (`eqm(S, f)[m]`, count > 2 × `eqLot(S, f)`). Prefer the supplier with the best relations; on a tie, the best model quality.
3. **The deal.** Move the kit with `stTake(S, f, q, m)` → `stPut(n, f, m, r.c, r.q)`, pay `eqPrice(S, f, m, q)` from buyer to seller, then call `eqRisk(S.id, n.id, f, m, r.c, price, false)`. Do not push to `G.armsT` yourself: `eqRisk` already records the deal, the spare-parts dependency, reputation and diversion, exactly as a player sale does. Read `function eqRisk(` first to confirm its argument order.
4. **When.** One call a month in `simulateMonth0`, right after `aiLendLease();`. Eligible buyers: alive, not human, not a horde, not an armed-group nation, with `warsOf(n.id).length`, or `n.posture === 'defend'`, or `n.mem && G.month - n.mem.w < 36`. At most one purchase per buyer every 3 months (`n.lastEqBuy`), price at most 25% of the buyer's gold. Each eligible buyer tries with a 30% chance a month, so it doesn't fire in lockstep.
5. **Log** one line for human-visible deals only (`log(..., 'econ', [n.id, S.id], 'econ')`) when either side is the player's nation or one of its allies; otherwise stay silent.
6. **New state:** `n.eqNeed` (transient, no `upgrade()` default needed), `n.lastEqBuy` (lazy).
