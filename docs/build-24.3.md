# Build 24.3 "Kutuzov" (2026-11-01): owner's changes

**UN meetings removed.** No General Assembly or Security Council session is ever held (`sumKeys` no longer lists `unga`; `sumEmerg`/`sumDefer` send any UN item to `unDirect`). `unDirect(it)` puts an aid, sanctions or condemnation item straight to a vote through `unPropose`, sponsored by its AI sponsor or the most fitting great power; ceasefires, peacekeepers, non-proliferation and the climate fund lapse. `UNASK = ['condemn', 'aid', 'sanctions']`: the player's UN panel (`unHTML`) and `unPropose` accept only these. Bloc, G7, G20 and BRICS summits are unchanged. Old saves: an open or queued UN session and pending UN items are dropped in `upgrade()`.

**Human players command manually only.** `armyMode` returns 'manual' for any army a human commands; `aiMilitary` does nothing for human nations (no automatic army moves, air missions or fleet missions); `planDay` skips human armies; commands `cmode` and `plan` (other than clearing) are refused for humans, `sugok` does nothing, `auto` always sets `G.autoBy` false. The army bar shows organisation, encirclement, the airborne drop (when the army can jump) and the division list; the Military tab's "Generals command idle units in wartime" checkbox is gone. Battle choices (`BCH`) are unchanged. The AI's armies still use modes and plans. Old saves: plans, modes and suggestions on human armies are cleared in `upgrade()`.

**Tested:** two months at war with Poland: Germany's armies, wings and fleets did not move or change missions on their own; plans and modes refused; a manual march to Niedersachsen arrived; AI armies moved exactly as in Build 24.2 (7 of 276 over two months, same seed). A year: no UN sessions; a condemnation passed and a sanctions vote failed as direct votes; the player's UN panel offers condemn, aid and sanctions; asking aid for Sudan passed; asking a ceasefire is refused. A Build 24.2 save with an open General Assembly and a German army on a front plan loaded with neither, and played six months.
